I am a Post-Baccalaureate Fellow at the [Centre for Responsible AI (CeRAI)](https://crai.iitm.ac.in), IIT Madras, where I work under [Prof. B. Ravindran](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=nGUcGrYAAAAJ&hl=en).  I am presently working on explainable ensemble audio deepfake detection for Indian settings to support fact-checkers and media houses.
Previously, I conducted my Master's thesis research at IIT Jodhpur in the [Image Analysis and Biometrics Lab (IAB)](https://iab-rubric.org/), under the guidance of [Prof. Richa Singh](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=okqK5UAAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Mayank Vatsa](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=-DAxp-cAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). My thesis was part of [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=qnlG3zPQUy) published in ICLR 2025.
Formally, I hold a BS-MS (Dual Degree) in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering from IISER Bhopal. I am broadly interested in Responsible AI. My research interests include but are not limited to evaluation and model editing for fairness and debiasing, unlearning, deepfake detection in audio, vision and multimodal media, multilingual and low-resource settings, and explainability.

Outside of research, I am an avid quizzer and sportsperson, and I enjoy watching movies and dramas. I am always on the lookout for opportunities to travel and explore new places.
