Hi there,

Currently an independent researcher and on a break. Previously I was a <b>Post-Baccalaureate Fellow</b> at the [Centre for Responsible AI (CeRAI)](https://cerai.iitm.ac.in), IIT Madras, where I worked under [Prof. B. Ravindran](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=nGUcGrYAAAAJ&hl=en). My work focused on <b>detecting textual, audio, visual, and multimodal deepfakes</b>, specifically in the <b>Indian context</b>. Before that, I did my external Master's thesis at IIT Jodhpur in the [Image Analysis and Biometrics Lab (IAB)](https://iab-rubric.org/), under the supervision of [Prof. Richa Singh](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=okqK5UAAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Mayank Vatsa](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=-DAxp-cAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate); the work on multimodal deepfake dataset contributed to a paper published at ICLR 2025.

I hold a BS-MS (Dual Degree) in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering from IISER Bhopal. My broader research interests lie in <b>Responsible AI</b>, spanning fairness and debiasing, model editing and unlearning, deepfake detection across audio, vision, and multimodal media, multilingual and low-resource learning, and explainability.

Outside of research, I am into a lot things. Recently ran my first half-marathon in Ghent, Belgium and official 10k at Bengaluru, India. I have also done couple of Himalayan treks (upto 15000ft). I enjoy watching movies, shows and reading books, and occasionally write reviews on Letterboxd and  and Goodreads. Lastly, like many others I live for traveling for new experiences.
